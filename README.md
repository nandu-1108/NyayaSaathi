### Legal Awareness for Everyone

**NyayaSaathi** is an AI-powered legal awareness platform designed to help people understand their legal rights, possible consequences, and practical next steps when facing everyday legal problems.

The platform allows users to describe their concerns in English or Kannada and receive easy-to-understand legal information.

## 🎯 Project Objective

Many people are unaware of their legal rights and the possible legal consequences of everyday situations.

NyayaSaathi aims to make legal awareness more accessible by explaining legal information in simple language and guiding users toward informed and safer decisions.

## ✨ Features

- **Bilingual Support:** Supports English and Kannada.
- **Problem Analysis:** Users can describe their situations in their own words.
- **Legal Awareness:** Provides relevant Indian laws and legal sections where applicable.
- **Possible Consequences:** Explains potential legal implications of a situation.
- **Practical Guidelines:** Suggests safer next steps and ways to understand and protect one's rights.
- **AI-Powered Responses:** Uses Google's Gemini API to generate understandable responses.
